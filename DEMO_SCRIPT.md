# Chalkline demo video script (about 2.5 minutes)

**Before recording:** open https://chalk-line-five.vercel.app in a fresh tab, light mode, mic allowed.
Have a phone photo or screenshot of a calculus problem ready, plus a sheet of paper.
Each section below lists what's on screen, then what to say.

---

**0:00 to 0:15. Hook (home screen)**
*On screen:* the home page, with the demo board solving by itself.
> "Most calculus apps just hand you the answer. Chalkline is an AI calculus tutor that shows you
> each step on a chalkboard, explains why, and then makes you do it yourself."

**0:15 to 0:35. Three ways in**
*On screen:* point at the three cards. Click **Upload a photo** and drop in the problem.
> "You can bring a problem three ways: snap a photo of your homework, type it with math keys,
> or just say it out loud. I'll upload a photo. Gemini reads it, and a SymPy math engine checks the solution."

**0:35 to 1:05. Walkthrough**
*On screen:* pick **Walkthrough**. Step through with the arrow. Turn on **read aloud** for one step.
> "Walkthrough builds the solution one line at a time. Each step has its rule and,
> right under it on the board, the reason why. With read aloud on, it explains the step
> and highlights each part as it talks about it. If I'm still confused, I can ask a
> follow-up question about this exact step."

**1:05 to 1:35. Step practice**
*On screen:* click **I'll write step N**. Type one wrong line, then a right one. Then use **Show me this step**.
> "But watching isn't learning, so Chalkline hands it back to you. I type my next step,
> and it checks the math itself, not just the wording. If I get it wrong, I get a hint instead of the answer.
> If I'm really stuck, 'show me this step' explains it on the board instead of just skipping ahead."

**1:35 to 2:00. Talk it through (voice)**
*On screen:* a library problem, then **Talk it through**. Turn on hands-free and say a step, a correction, and "next".
> "You can also solve out loud. I'll say 'u equals x squared, no wait, ln x'.
> It catches the correction and writes what I meant. Commands like 'next' or 'undo'
> work hands-free, and there's a built-in guide for how talking works."

**2:00 to 2:20. Check my work and the library**
*On screen:* **Problem library** with topics. Optionally show **Check my work** finding the first slip.
> "There's a library of solved problems across Calc 1 and 2, sorted by concept.
> And 'Check my work' finds the exact line where your own solution went wrong."

**2:20 to 2:40. Summary and close**
*On screen:* **See the summary**, then click the dark mode toggle.
> "At the end you get the whole solution with a comment on every line, the techniques used,
> and a similar problem to practice. It works on any device with no login, in light or dark mode.
> That's Chalkline: see the math move, then make it move yourself."

---

**Tips**
- Rehearse the voice section once. If the mic misbehaves on camera, cut it and show typing instead.
- Keep the browser at 100% zoom and full screen (F11) for a clean recording.
- Live link: https://chalk-line-five.vercel.app
